# Hyperframes Composition Brief: BD Retailers 90s Cinematic Walkthrough

## Objective
Create an in-depth 90-second motion graphics video depicting real e-commerce retailer scenarios in Bangladesh: from chaotic Messenger chats to an automated multi-tenant storefront, instant bKash checkout, Steadfast courier dispatch, Groq AI sales agent, and thriving merchant revenue.

## Output Details
- Output path: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (Landscape)
- Frame rate: 24 fps
- Duration: 90.0 seconds (2,160 frames)

## Palette & Design System
- Ground: `#070b14` (Midnight Deep Void)
- Card Surfaces: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)` with blur & `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`
- Accents:
  - Brand Emerald: `#10b981` / `#34d399`
  - Tech Cyan: `#06b6d4` / `#38bdf8`
  - Alert / Pain Crimson: `#ef4444` / `#f87171`
  - Payment Gold: `#f59e0b` / `#fbbf24`
  - Royal Violet: `#8b5cf6`

## Audio Plan
- Music: `assets/music/bgm.mp3` (Full 90-second duration, smooth fade out at 88.5s - 90s)
- Sound Effects: UI clicks, message pop sounds, payment chime, dispatch whooshes.
