# 🚀 Harvey: Autonomous B2B Retailer Acquisition Engine

Inspired by `ethanplusai/harvey`.
Provides automated, targeted outreach and onboarding playbooks tailored for Bangladeshi retail merchants, e-commerce entrepreneurs, and local physical stores.

## Key Workflows

### 1. Merchant Prospecting & Qualification
- Identify active F-commerce (Facebook shop) owners and local merchants facing pain points:
  - Losing orders in unorganized Messenger chats.
  - Manual courier booking errors.
  - Lack of automated SMS/Email/Push order tracking.
  - High monthly charges on complex SaaS tools.

### 2. High-Converting Localized Outreach (Bengali Copywriting)
- Direct, benefit-first messaging highlighting BD Retailers advantages:
  - "১ মিনিটে সম্পূর্ণ নিজস্ব অনলাইন শপ ও মোবাইল অ্যাপ তৈরি করুন।"
  - "মেসেঞ্জারে অর্ডার খোঁজার দিন শেষ — অটোমেটিক ইনভয়েস ও কুরিয়ার ট্র্যাকিং।"
  - "০% প্ল্যাটফর্ম ফি — কোনো হিডেন চার্জ নেই।"

### 3. Frictionless Onboarding Journey
- Guide retailers through 3 simple steps:
  1. Store name & custom subdomain selection (`shopname.bdretailers.com`).
  2. First 5 products catalog upload (via quick form or CSV import).
  3. One-click Facebook page connection for automated AI comment & DM replies.
