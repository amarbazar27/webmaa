## 1. What is BD Retailers?

BD Retailers (`bdretailers.com`) is a comprehensive digital retail ecosystem in Bangladesh. It serves two core functions:
1. **Consumer Marketplace**: A trusted multi-vendor marketplace where buyers purchase groceries, fashion, electronics, and lifestyle products with Cash on Delivery (COD) and verified nationwide delivery.
2. **Retail SaaS Platform**: A white-label storefront creation engine allowing Bangladeshi merchants, wholesalers, and small businesses to launch their own branded online store in 60 seconds.

---

## 2. Core Platform Features

- **Instant Store Launch (১ মিনিটে ফ্রি স্টোর)**: Create a fully customizable online shop with unique domain / subdomain, personalized banners, and product catalog in under a minute.
- **Steadfast Courier Automation**: Direct native integration with Steadfast Courier for automated consignment generation, real-time parcel tracking, and seamless cash-on-delivery settlement.
- **Bangladeshi Payment Gateways**: Built-in support for UddoktaPay, PipraPay, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, and Visa/Mastercard debit and credit cards.
- **Risk & Fraud Shield**: Machine-learning powered delivery risk analysis detecting suspicious orders and minimizing return-to-origin (RTO) rates.
- **White-Label Mobile App**: Automated Android APK / iOS application builds for tenant store brands with Google Sign-In and push notifications.
- **Zero Coding Required**: Intuitive mobile-first dashboard built specifically for Bangladeshi business workflows.

## 3. Merchant Plans & Pricing

- **30-Day Free Trial**: All new merchants receive 30 days of full, unrestricted access to the complete e-commerce suite.
- **Starter Plan**: Free forever tier with basic store cataloging and manual order management.
- **Pro Merchant**: Automated courier dispatch, custom domain support, WhatsApp business integration, and multi-staff accounts.
- **Enterprise Wholesaler**: Custom app build, dedicated account manager, bulk inventory sync, and priority API access.
